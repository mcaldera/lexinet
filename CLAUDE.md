# Sprawl (repo: lexinet)

Italian vocabulary trainer as a territory game. Words are nodes in a graph; answering a word correctly claims it and reveals its neighbours; memory (FSRS) decides when a word weakens and **supply** (what your words pass to each other along links, more the better you know them) decides what that costs. The author (Michael) is user #1 and iterates fast with feature requests.

`docs/PROPOSAL.md` is the original v0.1 plan. The project has since gone well beyond it at the author's request (tiers, verbs, supply game, claims, etymology, multiple word lists). Treat this file as the current source of truth; the proposal's "out of scope" list no longer binds. The supply game replaced an earlier influence game (control, hostile words, danger, landmarks, reach push) in Sep 2026: the author found three per-word numbers too confusing, but wants the network central (not plain flashcards). Later in Sep 2026 organic supply (every held word a source, by memory strength) replaced supply nodes earned every 12 words and placed by hand: the author found placing them too fiddly.

## Stack

- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 5 + Tailwind 3; graph: `react-force-graph-2d` (canvas); scheduling: `ts-fsrs` 4.x; tests: Vitest 2
- Fonts bundled offline via `@fontsource` (imported in `main.tsx`, families in `tailwind.config.js` and `visuals.ts`): Inter (UI, word names), Cinzel (Roman capitals: wordmark, region names, big titles), Cormorant Garamond italic (Latin roots, English cousins). The canvas redraws once they load.
- Tauri 2 shell in `src-tauri/` (thin, untested: this dev box has no Rust toolchain or webkit2gtk). Day-to-day the app runs in the browser.
- Persistence: SQLite. Tauri → `@tauri-apps/plugin-sql`; browser → `sql.js`, snapshotted to localStorage (key `sprawl.db`, or `sprawl-<graph>.db`; further runs `sprawl-<graph>~<run>.db`, see `src/runs.ts`)
- Offline pipeline: Python 3.13 in `pipeline/.venv` (sentence-transformers, networkx, pandas); never bundled

The output keeps the name `graph_oic.json` so existing runs keep their progress (ids = slug of `it`, or the explicit `id` of a renamed word). Edit the YAML, never the JSON. Linking rules: see Pipeline notes.
- The vocabulary is the only word list (the old CSV lists "My words" and "1000 frequent words" and their scripts were removed, Sep 2026). `build_graph.py`, `make_wordlist.py`, `split_levels.py`, `examples.py`, `conjugate.py`, `english_roots.py` and `layout.py` remain as libraries of `build_vocab.py`. The app picks `graph_oic.json` by default (`GRAPH_NAMES[0]`); `src/db/test-graph.json` is only the repo tests' fixture.
- Example sentences (`example {it, en, blank}` per node): source Tatoeba pairs from manythings.org (`~/.cache/sprawl/ita-eng.zip`, CC-BY 2.0 FR). A sentence qualifies only if it contains the word's base form and its English contains the gloss (rules out homographs like "la porta" = brings); simplest (rarest word most common), then shortest, wins. `build_vocab.py` does this itself (2257 of 2663 words).
- Sources behind the vocabulary (`data/sources/`): `common_2000.tsv` (the author's "2000 most used words" table, raw, with its machine-translation errors), `oic_scraped.csv` (the old onlineitalianclub.com scrape) and `oic_fixes.csv` (the first round of corrections). The vocabulary was built from both lists by a word-by-word review (Sep 2026): junk, phrases, ambiguous words (very different meanings), long glosses, rare derivatives and plural duplicates were dropped or merged; 673 left out, 33 merged. They are history now; the YAML is the truth.
- Node is 18 on the dev box: keep deps Node-18 compatible (Vite 5, Vitest 2, ts-fsrs 4; ts-fsrs 5 needs Node 20).

- Locate nodes by their colour in screenshots.
- Stop the server with `fuser -k 4173/tcp`. `pkill -f` can kill your own shell.

## Architecture

```
data/vocab/*.yaml ─ pipeline/build_vocab.py ─► data/graph_oic.json ─┐
data/words*.csv ── pipeline/build_graph.py ─► data/graph*.json ─────┴► app (import.meta.glob in src/graphs.ts)
                                                                        ├─ ?graph=<name> picks a graph; each has its own DB
                                                                        └─ SQLite holds progress only
```

- `graph.json` owns words, edges, regions, roots and conjugations; SQLite owns progress. `syncGraph` (repo.ts) runs on start: it adds new words and cards and repairs frontier states. Node ids are slugs of the Italian word, so regenerating a graph keeps progress (renaming a word loses it).
- `src/useSprawl.ts`: the state hook. Opens the DB, loads `AppData`, performs all mutations (`review`, `reviewTense`, plant, hints, time travel, reset), moves the horizon (`horizon(states, game, levels, t, memory?)`) and runs the siege (`advanceSiege`) when app time moves. `levelUpOf`: a new overall level, else the first topic that rose (`LevelUp.topic`: the banner says "Family: A1.3 open").
- `src/App.tsx`: UI orchestration (sessions, cooldowns, notices, selection, which side card shows). Big file; read it before changing flows.
- `src/progress.ts`: `computeProgress` (→ `Levels`: overall + per topic), the single source of truth for what may be revealed (`levelAllows`).
- `src/config.ts`: all tunables (`TOPIC_SHARE`, `OVERALL_SHARE`, cooldown, verb points per level, `SUPPLY`, claim gaps/expiry, raids).
- `src/supplyState.ts`: from `AppData` to the supply core: `siegeWorld(data, t)` (`output`: `wordOutput` of the word's strength level and its verb tenses as of `t`; `home`: the capital; break times: a miss or the weaker direction's recall at `SUPPLY.breakRecall`; hold times: memory stability, at least a day), `supplyNow` (flow, broken, cut; `input.sources` = what each word sends out now), `outputOf`/`isStronghold` (sends out 1, the capital aside), `seep` (fog from sources), `hangingOn` (what a word carries), `freshOutput`, `flowToward` (a word's top suppliers, and while it's short of supply the gain from winning each "?" within two links).

| `fallen` | `recordFalls`, `reclaimable`, `reclaim` (see Game rules) |
| `claim` | conquest in 3 fixed steps: 1 Recognise (it→en, right away), 2 Produce (en→it, ≥ `CLAIM_STEP_GAP_MS` later), 3 Lock in (either way, ≥ `CLAIM_RECALL_GAP_MS` later). **Fast track** (`known`): step 1 right at first sight (never shown) means you knew it, so step 2 conquers it, after `CLAIM_KNOWN_GAP_MS` (30 s: right after the Italian was on screen, producing it would be copying). `claimNext` says which card is open when; answers out of turn add nothing. `unseen` = no card ever answered (first sight). |
| `choices` | `meaningChoices`: the answer plus decoys of the same part of speech (same category first), never overlapping the answer ("dog"/"small dog") or each other |
| `cloze` | `cloze(example)`: the sentence before/after the gap (whole-word match) |
| `streak` | `dayKey` (local day), `streak` (current, alive through yesterday; best), `calendar` (weeks × Mon..Sun) |
| `expedition` | `planExpedition` (one of the 3 categories with the most "?"; goal ≤ words left; `EXPEDITION.minutes` app time), `expeditionStatus` (claimed/conquered since start count) |
| `raid` | `raidTargets` (supplied words drawn at random from a random 30 (weighing all is slow on a big map), weight 1 + half the words hanging on each: mostly plain words, now and then a chokepoint; the capital is never raided), `raidStarts` (none active, `gapMs` since last, `chance`), `defend`, `raidStatus` (won / lost with undefended `hit`: they break) |

| `queue` | automatic queue: due reviews (weakest first; App then puts chokepoints first, by `hangingOn`), then the unreviewed 2nd direction, then learning cards; `productionFirst`: when both directions of a word wait, en→it comes first (the it→en prompt shows the answer). `openOrder`: a clicked word's cards in a fixed order, the card whose prompt is already on show first (App's `shown` ref), so reclicking can't switch direction to peek. | `roots` | root hubs as hyperdrives: `rootCharge` (each family word counts the supply at it as a share of `SUPPLY.min`, up to 1), `rootOpenings` (charge ≥ `rootNeed` = `ROOTS.open` 3, or the whole family if smaller). `useSprawl.horizon` opens them (only hubs with a conquered word, i.e. visible) and turns all their hidden words into "?" at any level; meta `unlocked_roots`. No quiz. |
| `health`, `fade` | health = a word's recall, its weaker direction (`nodeHealth`); `forgetAt` = when that falls to a line. Names erode letter by letter from 90% (due) to 80% (broken, `NO_NAME`), then show "?"; `hintedName` keeps ~⅓ of the letters while an en→it card is due (`RECALL_NAME`). Bands: solid ≥ 90%, faded, dashed, cracked ≤ 80%. |
| `strength` | FSRS stability → level 0–5 ("memory strength", shown as blue dots with "lasts ~N", `MemoryDots` in NodeDetails; ★ are only for verb tenses); `nextPractice` (no cramming: practice only when due, `dueAt`). A cut-off word holds out as long as its stability (at least a day). `memoryOutlook` (NodeDetails/WordInfo `MemoryPanel`): due (90%), breaks (80%), since (last review), and the next level: how many right answers, each when due, and when (simulated with FSRS Good ratings). The level also sets what a word sends out (`SUPPLY.output`): a fresh conquest is level 2 (stability ~3.2 d), its first review (day 3) makes 3, ~day 14 4, ~day 49 5. |
| `game` | `GameNode` per word (meta `game`): `claim`, `fallen`, `missedAt` (a miss or undefended raid broke it until a right answer), `cutSince` (siege clock); `Game.simAt` = siege run up to. `neighbourWeights`, `without`. Older saves' influence fields are dropped on load (`parseGame`). |
| `supply` | **the supply network (organic, additive).** Every held, unbroken word is a source: `wordOutput` = `SUPPLY.output[strength level]` (0.05/0.1/0.25/0.4/0.65/1) + `perTense` 0.1 per learned verb tense, capped at 1; the capital (`home`) always sends 1. `supplyFlow`: per source a widest-path search (`spread`) through carriers (held, unbroken); each link passes on its strength (capped `SUPPLY.maxLink` 0.9); non-carriers receive but pass nothing on, or their `fog` share (the horizon); shares under `SHARE_MIN` (0.01) are dropped. **A word's supply is the sum of the shares of all its other words** (its own never counts, not even echoing back; the capital adds its own 1). A held word with ≥ `SUPPLY.min` (0.5) is supplied, else cut off. Runs on flat typed arrays (`linkIndex`, `NumHeap`) and keeps each source's shares (`Supply.shares`), so `hanging` (held words cut off if one broke: chokepoints) only recounts words that could drop below `min`, with only the sources whose supply passed *through* the word (`through`), and `advanceSiege` `reroute`s the same way at each break or fall (tests check both against full recounts). ~20 ms for 500 held words; one `hanging` ~3 ms. `contributions` (a word's suppliers: share and path), `gainIfWon` (what winning a word would add to another's supply: only the sources reaching the candidate are searched again; tested against a full recount), `coverage` (what a capital would reach if everything were held), `sourcesAt`, `advanceSiege` (event-ordered: breaks and falls in time order, so a week away plays out correctly; outputs change only at answers; cut-off words fall after their hold time), `horizonChanges` (hidden words next to anchors, or reached by summed fog supply ≥ `SUPPLY.reveal` 0.3, become "?"; lone untried "?" go back), `ghostWords` (fog supply ≥ `SUPPLY.sight` 0.12, below `reveal`: ghosts; less stays unseen). Simulated on the graph (scratch scripts, 6 words a day, walking to nearby words): 92–95% of held words supplied without a miss; one break cuts off up to ~5 (under 1 on average); a 3-word outpost with no link home never holds while fresh, 13% after its first review, 60% after two weeks. The horizon shows ~8 "?" beyond the first ring with 12 held, ~25 at 60, ~70 at 150 (strong territory sees further). |
| `tiers` | **levels** (`computeLevels`, `Levels`): every **topic** (category) climbs its own ladder of CEFR sub-levels (A1.1 … C1, tiers 1–10): its level k+1 opens at `TOPIC_SHARE` (0.6) of its own words of levels 1..k conquered (a level with none of its words passes at once), and only its words up to its level can be revealed (`levelAllows`). The **overall level** counts the words you hold anywhere: k+1 needs `OVERALL_SHARE` (0.25) of all words of levels 1..k (A1.2 at ~70 held, C1 at ~650) plus **verb points** `VERB_POINTS_PER_TIER` (4) × k (every learned, not faded tense of any verb is one point; capped by what the verbs in reach could give). A topic may run at most **one band** past the overall level (`cap`, bands from the level names via `bandsOf`: overall A1.x lets topics reach A2.2): a strict cap would have starved topics made of rarer words (Animals has 63 words, 1 at A1; 9 topics have none at A1.1). `TopicLevel.capped`: ready for its next level but held back (🔒 in the level panel). `openTierOf`: a word's topic level (planting prices). The author asked for this in Sep 2026: the old single level followed the home category and felt rushed (~5 words per level). |
| `verbs` | tense levels (presente → passato prossimo → imperfetto → futuro → condizionale → congiuntivo), one new tense per 20 h, `nextDrill`, `checkConjugation` |

### src/views

- `GraphView`: the canvas. Link tooltips say the two words, why they're linked and the link strength (%). Forces: charge −150 (unlimited range: a 300 px limit kept small categories near their link but squeezed the whole map towards the centre, and the author wanted the old layout back, Sep 2026), a collision force (`forceCollide` from `d3-force-3d`, radius 20 so names fit) and a gentle category pull (0.05), so a category spreads out instead of piling up. Layout is dynamic (the author preferred it to pinning everything): a dragged word stays where dropped. The whole network cools slowly (`d3AlphaDecay` 0.006, up to 2500 ticks) and then gets up to 3 `shake` rounds: words more than twice their region's spread from its centre jump back to it, everything gets a nudge, and the simulation reheats (escapes bad local optima). Each view (your map, the whole network) keeps its layout, pins and camera while you're in the other (`views`, `saveView`): coming back shows it exactly as left (`hold`: no re-run from full heat, which would drift it, as the map is never quite at rest); a view left before its layout finished carries on from there. The camera goes back where you had put it, else the view is fitted; during the whole network's first layout the camera follows it (every 60 ticks) until you move it. Words entering the view start next to a shown neighbour (`shownBefore`), never at a position left over from the other view (the whole network spreads words far apart). The whole network may zoom out to 0.04 (your map 0.15); its region names shrink when far out and are left out where there is no room. Remounted per seed (`key`). The capital and strongholds (words sending out full supply, `SupplyView.towers`) carry a gold tower (`drawTower`, the capital bigger; grey while broken). **Supply lights the territories** (`drawTerritories(…, lit)`): each word's patch of territory is weighted by its supply (`lightOf` = (`glowOf` / `LIGHT_FULL` 1.5)^1.5, capped; `glowOf`: a held word the larger of the supply reaching it and what it sends out (`sends`), 0 when cut off; others what seeps to them, App `reach` = `seep` from all sources); `densityField(…, weighted)` sums weight × kernel on the contour grid, so every point takes the average supply of the words whose ground it is. That light (region colour lifted towards white, up to `LIGHT_MAX` alpha, fading in over `LIGHT_RAMP` inside the border) goes into a small image drawn with `screen` under the terrace lines and the rim: well-supplied ground is bright, the thin fringe dim, cut-off pockets keep the bare wash. No separate glow around the words. Every supplied held word carries a **supply gauge**: a ring filled as far as the supply reaching it goes (full at the capital), orange below `THIN_SUPPLY` (0.7), gold below 100%, pale once full, at least ~2.5 px on screen at any zoom; zoomed in (scale > 1.5) its label adds "⚡ N%" (grey on cut-off words); cut-off words are drained grey with a siege ring (rose when broken); faint dashed "?" lie outside your supply; ghosts are faint shapes, clearer the more supply seeps to them, whose links to other ghosts stay hidden. The selected word (`activeId`) is drawn 35% bigger with a white target sized in screen pixels (k = 1/scale: halo, ring with four ticks, a pulse running outwards), so it is as plain zoomed far out as close up. The white rings (`activeId`) are always on the word the side panel is about (`panelId` in App: wait card > verb drill > word card > review card); the dashed yellow ring is the keyboard cursor when it differs. Draw order: CSS gradient background and a vignette div; `onRenderFramePre` draws the dot grid fixed to the map (`drawBackdrop`, a pattern) and the territories (`drawTerritories`); force-graph draws links and nodes; `onRenderFramePost` draws region names, then word labels, then edge arrows. **Territories** are vector contours (`contours.ts`, unit-tested: a density field of soft circles per region on a ~4 CSS px screen grid, marching squares, loops smoothed with quadratic curves): a wash, two terraces towards dense cores, a glow along the border and a crisp rim, all at full resolution (the old half-resolution bitmap was jagged). **Labels**: `drawNode` doesn't write names; it queues them (`label()` → `labels` ref) and `drawLabels` draws them last on a dark halo, by priority (selected, then stronger words, verbs), skipping any that would overlap one already placed or a bold region name, so a crowded map zoomed out shows fewer names instead of a smudge. Words are lit orbs (`orbGradient`, `softGlow`); supply lines (the link each supplied word gets its supply by, `supplyLine`) carry gold pulses from where the supply comes (`drawEnergy`, pace and phase from `hashOf` the ids). **Where supply comes from** (`flow`, `FlowView`, App `flowView` via supplyState `flowToward`): for the word the panel is about, its suppliers (from 0.5%, up to 12) get a gold ring and "+N%" (`drawFlow`, after the labels; lifted above a tower), the rest is summed ("incl. 18% from 5 farther words"), and the word itself shows its total ("151% of 50%", orange while short; not for the capital); all rounded together (`roundShares` in visuals.ts, largest remainder) so the badges add up to the total the panel shows. The links on their paths turn gold and carry pulses towards the word (`flowKey`, `supplyLine`); while it has less than it needs, each "?" within two links shows "+N% if won" in green (`gainIfWon` with a fresh conquest's output). Each named territory shows its topic's level under its name (`topicLevels`). Also: cohesion glow, keyboard cursor, attention ripples, edge arrows to off-screen attention words (`drawPointers`, clickable) and the jump bar.

- UI style: slim, dark glass. Component classes in `index.css`: `panel` (side cards), `glass` (things floating over the map), `btn-primary` / `btn-danger` / `btn-ghost`, `field` (inputs), `chip` (header counters); use them rather than ad-hoc slate/emerald classes. Banners over the map use `rise-in`. Header = logo mark (also `public/favicon.svg`) + name · level chip (tooltip has the details) · 🏆/🪙 · ☰. Notices are short toasts at the bottom of the map. `NodeDetails` shows a line or two and folds numbers (recall, supply, category links) under "more". No `(enter)` hints except on Next word; no explanatory paragraphs in cards.
- Helpers: `Etymology`, `visuals.ts` (shapes, link and band colours, `mixRgb`, font stacks), `contours.ts` (territory geometry), `useKeyboardCamera.ts`.
- `src/audio.ts`: music and sound effects synthesised with Web Audio (no files). Music is a slow D-major ambient loop (pads, sparse pentatonic plucks, reverb), off by default; effects (`sfx('select' | 'correct' | 'conquer' | 'miss' | …)`) are on by default. Header toggles; settings in localStorage `sprawl.audio`. The context starts on the first gesture and suspends in background tabs.

### Persistence

- Tables: `nodes(id, it, en, pos, theme, state)`, `edges`, `cards(id, node_id, direction, fsrs_json)`, `review_log`, `meta`.
- `cards.direction` is `it-en`/`en-it`, or `tense:<tense>` for verb tense cards (created on the first successful drill).
- Meta keys: `expedition` (current or null), `raid` ({active?, last}), `seed` (the capital: chosen first word; absent = picker shows unless reviews exist), `debug_day_offset`, `unlocked_roots` (JSON array), `game` (JSON: `simAt` plus per-node `{claim, fallen, missedAt, cutSince}`), `achievements` ({id: at}), `coins` (number; read from `seeds` for older saves), `user_edges` (planted links), `hints` (word ids). `landmarks`, `supply_nodes` and `best_held` are no longer read.
- In memory only (lost on reload): cooldown locks (app time, so time travel lifts them), notices, open sessions. Misses and siege clocks live in `game` (persisted).
- sql.js exports the whole DB on every `execute`, so batch writes (multi-row INSERT, one JSON value, `saveStates`: one CASE UPDATE for many state changes) instead of many small statements. One UPDATE per word made a 45-word horizon move block the page for 30+ s.

## Game rules (current)

- **Start:** a new game asks where to begin (a category, or anywhere), then (for a category) your **capital**: the first word, which always sends out full supply, chosen from the category's start candidates by how far its supply would reach (or by chance). Every topic has words up to A2, which the one-band rule opens from the start. Before its first conquest the capital shows only its neighbours.
- **Levels and runs:** each topic climbs its own level; the overall level (words held anywhere + verb points) lets topics run at most one band ahead (see `tiers`). Several runs per word list (menu → Runs).
- **Flow:** an idle card (nothing due here, or a word waiting/locked) offers "Next word" (Enter): an open claim step first, else the nearest "?".
- **Clicking empty map** lets go (App `clearSelection`, GraphView `onBackgroundClick`): no word selected (white rings and keyboard cursor gone; arrows resume from where the cursor was), open cards and panels closed (review card, word info, wait card, verb drill, plant picker, 🏆 and level panels). The panel rests (`deselected`: no due card jumps in, "N due elsewhere") until a word is picked; "Next word" still starts from the last word. In the whole-network view it clears the inspected word.

- **Claiming:** click a "?" (or move to it with the arrow keys) to get step 1, Italian → English. A correct answer makes it **claimed** (fragile). Step 2 (English → Italian) opens 1 min later and step 3 (either way) 5 min after that. A never-seen word's step 1 always starts typed; typed right at first sight = you knew it: step 2 opens 30 s later and conquers it (fast track). "☰ Choose instead" shows four meanings (keys 1–4) one way: no fast track and no going back to typing.
- **Examples:** reviews of conquered words into Italian are fill-the-gap when the word has an example (the gap's form or the full word both count); every revealed answer shows the example with 🔊.
- **Answers:** English alternatives separated by " / " are all accepted, and a note in brackets need not be typed ("clubs (card suit)" → "clubs"). A noun's plural (`pl`, one node for both forms) typed for the English prompt counts as almost ("Singular: …"); reveals and word cards show "pl. …".
- **Supply (core/supply.ts):** your words count only while the words around them hold them up. Every word you hold sends out supply by how well you know it (memory strength: 25% fresh, 40% after its first review, 65% after two weeks, 100% after ~7 weeks, a **stronghold** with a tower and a banner; verbs +10% per tense); the capital always sends 100%. Each word's supply flows along links through held, unbroken words, each link passing on its strength; a word gets the shares of all its other words added up and needs 50% (often well over 100% inside a territory). So new words need well-known words nearby, and a few linked words far from home hold each other once reviewed (an outpost). A held word **breaks** when you miss it (or an undefended raid hits it) or when its weaker direction's recall falls to 80% (its name has faded completely); a broken word passes nothing on, so what hangs on it is **cut off**: grey, no score, and it holds out as long as its memory lasts (at least a day) before falling back to "?" (win it back within 3 days with one right answer). One right answer mends a broken word and everything behind it lights up. Remedies: mend the gap, win or strengthen the words around it, plant a link around it. The details panel says how many words hang on a word; due reviews near you come chokepoints first. You can conquer outside your supply (faint "?"), but such words are cut off from the start.
- **Expeditions, raids, streak:** see `expedition`, `raid`, `streak` above. Raids strike after a right answer (15%, ≥ 20 min apart): up to 3 supplied words (chokepoints more likely, the capital never), 3 min to answer each right (raided words can always be practised, always en→it: the raid bar names them in English and the queue holds back their it→en card); undefended ones break; repelled = +3 coins. Expedition: 5 words of one category in 10 min, +10 coins. Clicks and the automatic queue only offer the open step. A never-seen word offers "Show me", which is not penalised.
- **Visual feedback:** territory grows with every claim step (and more on conquest and with verb levels). Claim steps, conquests and verb levels flash a burst on the map. A verb level also shows a gold banner, and the verb gets bigger with a gold outline and "★ tense" under its name. Link width follows the weight's rank in the graph (raw weights bunch up in 0.25–0.6). Gold pulses run along supply lines and a pale light lies where supply is strong (see GraphView). Conquered words show their root (pink) when zoomed in; etymology is in the status panel except while the word is being asked. The legend is in the guide.
- **Horizon and ghosts:** every hidden word next to one of your words (or a working capital) is a "?"; strong supply also seeps further (fog): words two links out, rarely three, open too (from words you hold: the better you know them, the further they see). A little seeping supply (4–8%, from any source) makes a ghost: faint, not conquerable yet (clicking one says so); none leaves a word unseen. Visibility, glow and supply are one scale. Untried "?" left without an anchor go back to hidden. A claimed word waiting for its next step has a white countdown ring.
- **Names are earned:** a word's Italian name appears on the map only once it is conquered. It is also hidden (map, tooltip, status panel) while it is the answer being asked. While an en→it card of it is due (or it is raided) the name shows only about a third of its letters (`hintedName`, `RECALL_NAME` in `core/fade`), so the map doesn't give the answer away before the card opens. Claimed words show "?" with a 3-segment ring.
- **Attention:** broken words (rose), raided words (orange), ready claim steps (green), due words (violet) and newly revealed words (light blue) ripple. Off-screen ones get arrows at the view edge, and the top-right jump bar or `N` cycles through them. A due card for an off-screen word pans the camera to it.
- **Score, achievements, coins:** header 🏆 score · 🌱 coins opens the progress panel (score per category (supplied words only), achievements). Coins: +`COINS_PER_CONQUEST` per real conquest (not a win-back) plus achievement rewards. **Planting** (from `PLANT_COST` coins, more for later levels): on a conquered word's details, "🌱 Plant a word" opens `PlantPicker` (options by English meaning). The chosen hidden word becomes "?" next to it, joined by a player link (`via: 'user'`, meta `user_edges`, merged into `data.edges`, so it carries supply and counts for the horizon and score: a planted detour protects a chokepoint). Drawn as a green dash-dot link.
- **Hints:** for `HINT_COST` coins a visible word shows its English cousin (`cognate`) as a small amber node tied to it (meta `hints`, not an edge: no score, no supply). Words without a curated `cousin` get one from the pipeline (`pick_cognate`: the root's English descendant that looks like the word and relates to its meaning; precision over coverage).
- **Lost is not forgotten (`core/fallen.ts`):** a conquered or claimed word that falls back to "?" (siege, expired claim, a miss on a claim) keeps `fallen {was, steps, at}` on its game node. For `RECLAIM_MS` (3 days app time) one right answer restores it as it was (conquered, or the claim with its steps done as of now); teal ↺ ring on the map, attention kind `reclaim`, "Winning back" banner. After that it's fully lost.
- **Misses:** a real miss locks the word for 60 s (app time); a held word breaks (`missedAt`, see Supply) and the notice says how many words it cut off; a claimed word falls back to "?". Unfinished claims expire after 24 h.
- **Conquered words:** clicking or jumping to one practises only its due cards (or its weakest card when it's fading but not due), never the not-due other direction. Conquest graduates both FSRS cards (the unanswered direction gets a Good too, unlogged via `saveCard`), so a new conquest isn't asked again at once. They can be practised only when due, broken or raided (the status panel shows "due in"). Clicking a solid one opens `WordInfo`.
- **Tiers** (levels) come from word frequency or a `tier`/`level` column (CEFR names in `graph.params.tier_names`). Verb points gate the overall level (see `tiers`); the header level chip opens the level panel with topics and verbs.
- **Roots** shared by 2+ words are hexagon hubs, shown once one of their words is conquered. A sealed hub charges (pink ring) as its words come inside your supply and opens by itself at 3 (see `roots`): its English cousins show and all its words become "?" anywhere on the map (a hyperdrive). Clicking a hub shows a wait card with its charge or meaning.
- **Map:** the layout is organic (force simulation). Invisible same-category springs gather discovered words, and territories are contours of the words' summed fields (fused blobs) whose size grows with word strength.

## Pipeline notes

- **Meaning score:** ConceptNet Numberbatch on the English gloss (hyphens become underscores: `mother_in_law`, `great_grandmother` are phrase vectors), which captures association (dog→bark). Sentence-embedding models only capture likeness and did worse in a labelled benchmark; `--model BAAI/bge-m3` still works.
- **Categories:** the vocabulary's 44 files (`vocab.CATEGORIES`, map order = related categories adjacent), every word in exactly one. CSV lists: their themes; untagged words join a theme only if they clearly fit (`THEME_FIT` 0.45, `THEME_MARGIN` 0.1), else stay **independent** (`region: null`).
- **Vocabulary links (`build_vocab.py`):** (1) per category a tier-respecting spanning tree plus each word's top 2 partners there from 0.35 (score 0.7 meaning + 0.5 family + 0.15 same category); (2) families: a shared root always, a shared stem only when the meanings relate (≥ 0.25: la cucina/cucinare yes, la casa/il caso no), each member linked to its closest more common relative; (3) at most one direct bridge per word to another category, same part of speech, meaning ≥ `BRIDGE_MIN` 0.55, never when the two English glosses share a word (English homographs: il diritto "right"/la destra "right", il disegno "drawing"/trarre "to draw") or rest on an ambiguous English word (`AMBIGUOUS`: will, can, show, close…); (4) categories joined into one map by their best low-tier bridges; (5) **male/female pairs** (`counterparts`: any meaning of the gloss swaps into one of the other's, mother↔father, "child / little boy"↔"little girl" via `GENDER_PAIRS`, or Italian -o/-a, -e/-a, -tore/-trice, -e/-essa with the same main meaning, il cugino/la cugina "cousin"; both kinds of evidence together win) are linked and share their links: a link to another pair member is mirrored onto its counterpart; a link to an unpaired word is copied unless it is a family (root/stem) link (la sorella – la suora stays hers) or that word's English is gendered (la figlia – la signorina "young lady"); (6) YAML `links`/`unlink` win. `via`: tree/theme/family/bridge/manual. Tiers: each word's CEFR `level` (or its frequency: `vocab.word_rank` = most common inflected form in subtitles, `LEVEL_BY_RANK`) split into 10 even sub-levels A1.1…
- **CSV-graph links (`build_graph.py`):** score = 0.6 semantic + 0.25 same stem + 0.25 shared root + 0.15 same theme. Edges are a tier-respecting maximum spanning tree plus top-3 bonus links scoring ≥ 0.25. Lists with themes first assign untagged words to a theme, then add in-category links (`via: 'theme'`): a tier-respecting spanning tree per category plus each word's top 2 partners there from 0.3, so no category falls apart (OIC went from 266 pieces in 31 categories to 31). In the app, links between categories are twice as long and 0.3× as strong in the force layout. Lists with themes use them as regions, else Louvain communities (`layout.py`).
- **English cousins (hints):** a generated cousin must look like the word *and* evoke its meaning (Numberbatch similarity to the gloss ≥ `COGNATE_MIN_RELATED` 0.18, and not be the gloss itself): pancia → panzer, testa → test, letto → lecture are out. Root hubs keep only English words related to the root's meaning or one of its words (`HUB_MIN_RELATED`).
- **Etymology:** hand-curated columns in `words.csv` (root, root_lang, root_meaning, cognate, cognate_note). Generated lists use Wiktionary (kaikki.org) with deliberately conservative root-family rules: homographs produced wrong families, and a wrong etymology is worse than none.
- **Conjugations:** `conjugate.py` reads Wiktionary forms. Stress accents are normalised, passato prossimo is built from auxiliary + participle, reflexives are handled, and `-sene` verbs are skipped.
- **Caches** (~1.3 GB, auto-downloaded) live in `~/.cache/sprawl/`: Numberbatch, kaikki Italian/Latin/English, frequency lists, pickled indexes. Delete an `*.pkl` to rebuild its index.
- `data/vocab/`, `data/sources/` and `graph_oic.json` are git-ignored (partly derived from the onlineitalianclub.com scrape and a third-party frequency table: personal study only). Whether to track the vocabulary in git is the author's call.
